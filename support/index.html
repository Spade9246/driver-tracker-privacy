Driver's Log Support

Need help with Driver's Log? Email arthoriusnetwork@gmail.com.

To help us understand the issue, include:

  • Your phone model and iOS or Android version
  • Your Driver's Log app version
  • What happened and what you expected
  • A screenshot if useful, with private information hidden

Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or tax identification numbers.

Backups

Google sign-in is optional and is used for backup to your own Google Drive account. If you're having trouble with backups, describe any error you see—don't share your Google password.

Tax estimates

Canadian tax estimates cover federal tax only, not provincial or territorial income tax. Estimates and T2125 drafts are planning tools, not tax advice or an official filing.

Privacy

For how Driver's Log uses data, and for privacy requests, see the Privacy Policy.
