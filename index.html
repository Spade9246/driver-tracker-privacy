Privacy Policy for Driver's Log

Last updated: November 5, 2026. Describes the app as of version 1.0.0+23.

This policy explains what data Driver's Log ("the App") collects, how it is used, and where it is stored. Your trip, financial, vehicle, and profile records are kept on your device. There is no Driver's Log password account. We do not sell your data.

Some features send limited data off the device. The App can sign you in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication, write coarse presence to Cloud Firestore if you turn on Nearby Drivers, send short prompts to Cloud Functions and Google's Gemini when you ask for an AI feature, and, if you choose it, back up your records to the hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. Crash reports, optional usage analytics, ads, and store purchases are described below.

Analytics events, crash reports, and AI requests are not a copy of your full trip log. They do not include your receipt photos or saved voice recordings. Location does leave the device in the limited cases below: Nearby Drivers presence, a fuel-price area you request, and any backup you turn on.

Location

While a shift is active, the App reads GPS on your device to calculate mileage and detect trip start and end points. Those tracks stay in the App's private storage on the device unless you back them up yourself.

Location is not limited to the device in every case. If you turn on Nearby Drivers, the App writes a coarse area code (a geohash) so it can show an approximate count of other drivers. A fuel-price lookup you start may send the area you are asking about. The App does not upload your full route to us as a trip log for those features.

Nearby Drivers and Anonymous Sign-In

Nearby Drivers is off until you turn it on and confirm. When it is on, the App signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication. That creates an anonymous user id. It is not your name or email.

The App then sends coarse presence to Cloud Firestore: a geohash area rather than a precise point or street address. Presence is aggregated so the feature can show a nearby count. It is not a public map of identifiable people. You can turn Nearby Drivers off in Settings. Turning it off stops new presence updates.

The same anonymous id may be used so this install can remember optional Pro status. It is not a password account for your logbook.

Records on Your Device and Google Drive Backup

Trip, revenue, expense, shift, vehicle, and profile records are stored in the App's private storage on your device. They power totals, the tax estimates shown in the App, and exports you start (including CSV).

Drive backup is optional. If you sign in with Google, the App requests the drive.appdata scope and stores a backup in the hidden app-data folder of your Google Drive. We do not keep a separate copy of that backup on our own servers. The App can read and write that backup only while you are signed in with that Google account. Signing out stops new backups. It does not by itself delete a backup already in your Drive.

Your phone's own automatic backup (Android backup or an iCloud device backup) is controlled by the operating system and your store account. We do not operate that backup.

Receipts and Voice Entry

Receipt photos are read with on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit). The App does not upload the photo. The photo is not kept after the scan.

Voice entry uses your device's speech-recognition service to turn speech into text you can correct before saving. Depending on the device and its settings, that service may process audio on the device or send it to Apple or Google to produce the transcript. The App does not keep the audio. Saved entries are the text you confirm, stored with your other records.

AI Features, Cloud Functions, and Gemini

These features run only when you ask for them. A Firebase Cloud Function receives a short prompt and, for the AI features, passes it to Google's Gemini:

  • AI Insights sends an aggregated summary (such as total revenue, expense category totals, total miles, and mileage statistics for a recent period). Individual trip or transaction rows are not sent.
  • OBD explain sends the diagnostic code you ask about, not your trip history. The Bluetooth link to an OBD-II adapter stays between your phone and the adapter.
  • Fuel prices may send the area you are looking up so a price result can be returned.

These calls are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and App Tracking

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded ads that can unlock features. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, device identifiers, and ad-related usage data. On iOS, the App uses App Tracking Transparency and asks before tracking across apps and websites.

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Privacy > Ads or the iOS tracking prompt). AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Optional purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment details stay with the store. We cannot see your card or bank number. Store billing is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice or Apple's privacy policy, depending on where you bought the App. The App may keep a local Pro flag and associate it with the anonymous Firebase user on that install.

Crash Reports and Analytics

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (device model, OS version, app version, and a stack trace) so bugs can be fixed. It is not a copy of your trips, receipts, or voice recordings.

Firebase Analytics can collect basic usage (screens opened, feature use, app opens). You can turn analytics off in the App's Settings. It is not your financial ledger.

Firebase and Google services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Google keeps that data under each product's retention settings. Analytics retention is often up to 14 months, depending on the property settings.

Permissions

  • Location (including background) — shift and mileage tracking, and coarse presence only if Nearby Drivers is on.
  • Camera and Photos — receipt scanning and a vehicle photo.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — voice entry.
  • Notifications — a reminder while a shift is active, and maintenance reminders.
  • Bluetooth — optional OBD-II adapters.
  • Advertising and tracking — AdMob, and the iOS tracking prompt.

A permission is requested when the feature is first used. If you decline it, the rest of the App still opens and that feature stays off.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Account and Data Deletion

You can remove on-device records yourself, turn off Nearby Drivers, sign out of Drive backup, and uninstall the App. Firestore presence and the anonymous Firebase user are not removed by uninstalling alone. The steps, and how to ask us to delete server-side records we can match, are on the data deletion page.

Email arthoriusnetwork@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request — Driver's Log. We will respond within 30 days. Include the Google account email if you used Drive backup, and about when you used Nearby Drivers, so we can look for a matching anonymous id. If an email does not match an anonymous id, we will say so and delete the records we can identify.
