Delete My Account & Data — Driver's Log

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Driver's Log keeps your trip, financial, vehicle, and profile records on your device. That is not the only copy that can exist. If you turn on backup, a copy is stored in your own Google Drive app data. If you turn on Nearby Drivers, the app creates an anonymous Firebase user and writes coarse presence to Cloud Firestore. This page explains how to delete each of those.

Option 1 — Delete data on the device

Step 1. Open Driver's Log on your device.
Step 2. Open Settings and turn Nearby Drivers off. That stops new presence updates to Firestore.
Step 3. If you use Drive backup, sign out of it in Settings. Signing out stops new backups. It does not delete a backup already stored in your Google account.
Step 4. If Settings includes Account & Data → Delete all data, use it and confirm. That removes trips, expenses, revenue entries, vehicles, and profile data stored on the device.
Step 5. Uninstall the app to remove the remaining on-device database. Uninstalling does not delete Firestore presence, the anonymous Firebase user, or a Drive app-data backup.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send a deletion request to: arthoriusnetwork@gmail.com

Put Data Deletion Request — Driver's Log in the subject. If you signed in for Drive backup, include that Google account email. If you used Nearby Drivers, include about when you used it. We will respond within 30 days and delete Firestore presence and the anonymous Firebase user when we can match them to your request.

The anonymous id is not your email address. If we cannot match the request to a record, we will tell you. Turning Nearby Drivers off and uninstalling the app stops further collection from that install.
